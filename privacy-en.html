Privacy Policy

Chốn lành chờ bạn · Updated 9 November 2026

Chốn lành chờ bạn is a free online study room built and run by one individual. This page explains what the website collects, how it is used and what rights you have over your data. If this English version differs from the Vietnamese version, the Vietnamese version applies.

1. Data we collect

  • When you sign in with Google: the name, email address and profile picture Google provides. The website only requests basic profile access (email, profile). It does not read your Gmail, Drive or other Google data.
  • When you use “Quick start”: only the display name you choose. The account is tied to the browser you are using.
  • Your display name on the website.
  • Profile (optional): avatar, color, country, education level and school that you choose to fill in.
  • Friends and private messages: friend requests, your friend list, private messages between two people, room invites, kudos you send and receive, and challenges you join.
  • Rooms you create: name, description, tags and background. Room passwords are stored only as a one-way hash; nobody can read the original password.
  • Messages, reactions and reports you send in room chats.
  • Study time: seconds of focus and number of completed sessions per day.
  • Your status (focusing, on a break, idle) while the website is open. This exists only while the page is open and is not stored.
  • Camera and screen sharing: your camera and shared screen travel directly between devices in the room. They do not pass through and are not stored on the website’s servers. To connect directly, devices in the room may see each other’s network (IP) address. Background effects (blur, image background) run on your own device.
  • In your browser (localStorage): settings, goals, chosen background, language and cached study stats. When you sign in, unclaimed guest study history is imported into that account; new study time syncs to Supabase. Settings and goals stay in your browser.
  • Study day: stats, streaks, challenges and rankings use UTC+7 (Vietnam time) so members share one study calendar.

2. How we use it

  • To sign you in and show your name to others in the room.
  • To show messages, the number of people studying and each person’s study time in shared rooms.
  • To save your study stats so you can see them on any device.
  • To keep rooms safe: prevent spam, handle reported messages and ban accounts that break the rules.

The website does not sell data, does not run ads and does not use your data to track you on other websites.

3. Who can see your data

  • Anyone visiting the website (even without signing in) can read messages in public rooms, plus the display names and today’s study time of people who are online. Do not post personal information such as phone numbers, addresses or your school in chats.
  • The weekly and monthly leaderboards publicly show the display name and study time of signed-in users.
  • Your profile (display name, avatar, country, education level, school) and your name in the community member list are public.
  • Private messages can only be seen on the website by the two people in the conversation. Only friends can send each other private messages.
  • Your email address is never shown to other users.
  • Admins can see reported messages and may delete messages or ban accounts that break the rules.

4. Third-party services

  • Supabase: stores accounts, messages and study time.
  • Google: Google sign-in; Google Fonts for typography.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: bot protection for “Quick start”.
  • YouTube: plays the background videos and music you choose. YouTube may record views under Google’s policies.
  • Unsplash / Picsum: background images.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website.

Each service processes data under its own policy.

5. Retention

Data is kept until you ask for your account to be deleted. Messages you delete in a chat have their content removed immediately.

6. Your rights

  • Change your display name at any time (click your initial in the top right → Account).
  • Delete your own messages.
  • Ask to see, correct or delete your whole account and data by emailing the address in section 8. Requests are handled within 30 days.

7. Young users

The website is made for students. If you are under 16, please ask a parent or guardian before creating an account. Parents can email us to have their child’s account deleted.
